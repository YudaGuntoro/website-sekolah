# Restorasi logo Cendekia

Logo PNG transparan 150 × 150 yang diekstrak dari brosur dipertahankan di `Frontend/public/cendekia-logo.png`. Versi restorasi untuk antarmuka adalah `Frontend/public/cendekia-logo-hd.png` (1254 × 1254, transparan), dibuat dengan tool imagegen bawaan. Ini merupakan restorasi berbantuan AI dari logo raster, bukan master vektor resmi sekolah. Sumber lama tetap disimpan untuk pembandingan.

Komponen logo menampilkan PNG baru langsung tanpa kompresi optimasi Next.js. Ikon browser diturunkan dari sumber HD.

## Prompt yang digunakan

Restore and upscale this exact existing school emblem for production website use. This image is the EDIT TARGET, not inspiration for a new design. Produce one centered high resolution 1024x1024 PNG badge, genuinely transparent outside the circular border, with no padding beyond a very small transparent margin. Preserve the logo's exact proportions, composition, blue/cyan/yellow/white/black colors, cyan outer rim, yellow ring, black thin inner rim, left-to-right white-to-cyan circular background gradient, white minaret with round cap on the left, white mosque dome with black thin outlines and cyan crescent accent, cyan shield/ribbon with yellow trim and black crenellated bridge, and white/cyan horizontal waves. Restore edges and smooth pixelation. Faithfully restore the exact black condensed bold serif text 'SMPIT CENDEKIA' in a straight horizontal cyan nameplate, and exact yellow uppercase serif text 'PURWAKARTA' curving upward around bottom shield. Make the two text labels clearly readable and accurately spelled. Keep every original design element and its placement. No redesign, no added decorative elements, no shadows, no mockup, no extra text, no halo, no white rectangular background.
